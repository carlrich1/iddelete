How it works

We do the boring, infuriating work for you.

Behind the cheerful name is a serious, fully automated removal pipeline running 24/7. Here's exactly what happens after you sign up.

1

You give us your identifiers

During signup we collect the bare minimum needed to find you on broker sites: full legal name, any previous names (maiden names matter — brokers love them), current and past addresses, phone number, and email. Adding more aliases finds more listings, but is always optional.

2

Our scanners crawl 200+ brokers

Within the first hour, our headless browser fleet searches every covered data broker for any combination of your identifiers. Each match is verified (we won't request removal for someone who isn't you) and saved to your exposure report.

3

Removal requests get filed

For each verified listing, our system files the exact opt-out request that broker requires. Some have web forms, some require notarized affidavits, some still require fax. We handle all of it. You'll see each request appear in your dashboard with its status.

4

We confirm every removal

When a broker confirms, we mark it removed. When a broker is slow, we re-send. When a broker is non-compliant, our specialists escalate using applicable state law. You see "Removed ✓" — we handle the back-and-forth.

5

We re-scan every month

Brokers re-add you. It is not a one-time job. Every 30 days (every 7 on Pro) we re-scan all 200+ sites. If you reappear anywhere, the removal cycle restarts automatically without you lifting a finger.

6

You see everything

Your dashboard shows every broker, every record, every request status, and every removal date in real time. Want to add a phone number? Move to a new address? Add a family member? Two clicks.

What we never do

  • Sell your data. We are a privacy company. Selling your data would be a betrayal so absurd it would end the company. We don't.
  • Share with advertisers or AI trainers. Your identifiers are used only to submit removals, then encrypted at rest.
  • Keep what you don't need us to keep. Cancel anytime and we delete your records within 30 days.
  • Hide our methods. Every broker we cover and every request we file is visible in your dashboard.
