Common questions

Real talk.

Is this legal?

Absolutely. State laws (CCPA in California, CDPA in Virginia, etc.) and the brokers' own published policies require them to remove your information when you ask. We just ask — at scale, on your behalf, in the right format, every single time.

Do you sell my data?

No. We are literally a privacy company. We use your information only to identify you on broker sites and submit removals. We do not share it with advertisers, partners, AI training datasets, or anyone else. Ever.

How long until I disappear?

Most brokers honor removals within 7–45 days. You'll see the first batch disappear in two weeks. After ~60 days, the majority are gone. Then we keep going monthly because brokers love to re-add you.

What if a broker ignores the request?

We escalate. Most states give us a legal cause of action against brokers who ignore valid deletion requests. Our specialists handle it; you don't have to do anything.
