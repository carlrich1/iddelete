FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

The basics

Is this legal?

Yes. State privacy laws (CCPA, CDPA, CPA, UCPA, CTDPA, etc.) and the brokers' own published policies require them to delete your data when you request it. We just submit those requests on your behalf, in the exact format each broker requires, and follow up if they don't comply.

Do you sell my data?

No, and we'd close the company before we did. We use your identifiers only to find you on broker sites and submit removals. No advertising, no analytics partners with PII, no AI training, no resale, ever. Read the privacy policy.

How is this different from doing it myself?

Functionally, it isn't — you have the right to file every one of these requests yourself, for free. Practically, it would take you 200+ hours to file them initially and then about 10 hours/month forever to re-do them. We do all of it for $9/month.

How is this different from DeleteMe / Incogni / Kanary?

We cover the same kinds of brokers and use the same legal mechanisms. We're cheaper, our dashboard is friendlier, our family plan includes more people for less money, and our re-scan cadence is faster. Try our free scan — you'll see the difference.

Timing & results

How long until I'm actually removed?

First removals usually appear within 7–14 days. Most brokers comply within 45 days (the longest deadline allowed by most state laws). After ~60 days, the majority of your initial listings are gone. Then we keep re-scanning monthly because brokers re-list you.

Will my spam calls stop immediately?

Not immediately — call centers buy lists in bulk and you'll be on lists already in circulation. But over 2–3 months as old lists age out and new lists no longer include you, most subscribers see a significant drop. Many report near-zero by month 4–6.

What if a broker ignores the request?

We resend. If they still ignore us past the statutory deadline, our privacy specialists escalate — sometimes a complaint to the state AG, sometimes a direct legal demand letter. Most brokers fold immediately. The few that don't, we document and report.

Privacy & security

Why do you need so much personal info?

Brokers identify you by combinations of name + DOB + address + phone. To find every listing of you, we need to search every combination. We collect the minimum needed for that, encrypt it at rest, and never share it.

What about my kids?

The Family plan covers children with the guardian's consent. Kids actually have more leverage — most state laws give minors broader deletion rights, and several brokers must remove them on request without verification.

Where is my data stored?

Encrypted at rest in US-based, SOC2 Type II data centers. Identifiers are encrypted with per-customer keys. Production access is logged and audited.

What's the refund policy?

If we haven't started your initial scan, full refund. After we've started removals, we refund the unused portion of your plan.

Do you offer business / employee plans?

Yes. Contact us for team pricing. Common for high-risk roles like nurses, social workers, judges, journalists, and executives.
